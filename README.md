# Helpful Hand University Guide

Create a website called "Helpful Hand" for young high school graduates who need help with university admissions.



The purpose of the website is to help students choose universities and study programs based on their high school exam results, interests, and future career goals.



The website should work as an intelligent university recruitment search engine.



Main features:



1. University search:

- Allow users to search for Polish and international universities.

- Include both public and private universities.

- Display university admission requirements.

- Show required subjects, minimum scores, recruitment rules, and additional criteria.



2. Candidate profile and results:

Users should be able to enter:

- Their high school final exam (Matura) results.

- Subjects they took and their scores.

- Additional achievements that may give extra points, such as competitions, projects, certificates, or other activities.



Based on this information, the search engine should show universities and study programs where the candidate matches the admission requirements.



3. Career and study preferences:

Users should be able to enter:

- Their future career goal (for example: doctor, lawyer, engineer, psychologist).

- Their preferred field of study.



The system should suggest suitable university programs and explain why they match the user's profile.



4. University matching system:

The search results should include:

- Universities in Poland and abroad.

- Public and private institutions.

- Study programs that match the user's results.

- Admission requirements for each program.

- Information about which subjects are important for recruitment.



5. University details:

Users should also be able to search for a specific university and see:

- Admission requirements.

- Required Matura subjects.

- Recruitment rules.

- Recommended subjects to take during high school.



6. Helpful advice:

The website should provide suggestions such as:

- Which subjects are worth choosing for the Matura exam.

- How to improve admission chances.

- What study programs match the user's interests and results.



Design requirements:

- The entire website must be in Polish language.

- Polish language is the most important requirement.

- The website name is: "Helpful Hand".



Visual style:

Use a modern, elegant design with:

- Dark purple colors.

- Burgundy/red accents.

- Light beige backgrounds.

- A friendly and trustworthy appearance suitable for young students.



The website should feel like a supportive guide for students during the university recruitment process.



Include a database of Polish universities with their admission requirements.



The goal is to create a helpful platform that acts as a "helping hand" for students choosing their future education path.
